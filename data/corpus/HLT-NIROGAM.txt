SECTION 1. PREAMBLE

1.1 Operative Clause
Sentinel Health Assurance Limited agrees to indemnify the Insured Person against Medical
Expenses incurred during the Policy Period in respect of Illness or Injury, subject to the Sum
Insured, the terms set out herein and the particulars stated in the Policy Schedule, in
consideration of the premium received.

SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 Pre-Existing Disease
Pre-Existing Disease means any condition, ailment, injury or disease diagnosed by a Medical
Practitioner within thirty-six (36) months prior to the effective date of the first Policy
issued by the Insurer, or for which medical advice or treatment was recommended by or received
from a Medical Practitioner within thirty-six (36) months prior to that date.

2.2 Hospital
Hospital means an institution established for in-patient care and day care treatment which is
registered with the local authorities, has qualified nursing staff under its employment round
the clock, has at least ten (10) in-patient beds in towns with a population of less than ten
lakhs and fifteen (15) in-patient beds elsewhere, has a fully equipped operation theatre of its
own, and maintains daily records of patients.

SECTION 3. BENEFITS

3.1 In-Patient Treatment
The Insurer shall indemnify reasonable and customary Medical Expenses incurred on in-patient
treatment of the Insured Person, including room and nursing charges, consultant fees, operation
theatre charges, drugs, implants and diagnostics, up to the Sum Insured stated in the Policy
Schedule.

3.2 Pre and Post Hospitalisation
Expenses incurred in the thirty (30) days preceding Hospitalisation and the sixty (60) days
following discharge shall be indemnified, provided they relate to the condition for which the
admitted Hospitalisation claim was made.

3.3 Domiciliary Hospitalisation
Where treatment that would ordinarily require Hospitalisation is taken at home because the
condition of the Insured Person prevents removal to a Hospital, or because a hospital bed was
unavailable, such expenses shall be indemnified provided the treatment continues for at least
three (3) consecutive days.

3.4 Annual Health Check-Up
The Insured Person shall be entitled to a preventive health check-up at a Network Provider at
the end of every two (2) continuous claim-free Policy Years, up to one percent (1%) of the Sum
Insured subject to a maximum of Rs. 7,500.

3.5 Road Ambulance
Ambulance charges for transporting the Insured Person to the nearest Hospital following an
Emergency shall be reimbursed up to Rs. 3,000 per event.

SECTION 4. WAITING PERIODS

4.1 Initial Waiting Period
No benefit shall be payable in respect of Illness contracted within the first thirty (30) days
of the Policy Inception Date, other than claims arising from an Accident.

4.2 Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period
Expenses related to the treatment of a Pre-Existing Disease and its direct complications shall
be excluded until the expiry of twenty-four (24) months of continuous coverage from the
inception of the first Policy with the Insurer.

4.3 Specified Ailments
A waiting period of eighteen (18) months shall apply to cataract, hernia, piles, sinusitis, gall
stones, kidney stones, hysterectomy and surgery of the knee or hip other than that necessitated
by an Accident.

4.4 Maternity
Maternity Expenses shall be payable after twenty-four (24) months of continuous coverage,
limited to Rs. 50,000 for a normal delivery and Rs. 75,000 for a caesarean section, and
restricted to the first two living children.

SECTION 5. LIMITS AND CO-PAYMENT

5.1 Room Rent
Room and nursing charges shall be restricted to a Single Private Air-Conditioned Room. Where a
higher category is availed, the Insurer shall be liable only in the proportion that the eligible
room rent bears to the actual room rent charged.

5.2 Zone-Based Co-Payment
Where the Insured Person obtains treatment in a city classified by the Insurer as Zone A but has
paid premium applicable to Zone B, a co-payment of twenty percent (20%) shall apply to the
admissible claim amount. No co-payment applies where treatment is taken within the zone of the
premium paid.

SECTION 6. EXCLUSIONS

6.1 Dental Treatment
Dental treatment or surgery of any kind, unless necessitated by an Accident and requiring
Hospitalisation, is excluded. Routine examinations, scaling, filling and orthodontic treatment
are not payable under any circumstances.

6.2 Obesity and Weight Control
Treatment for obesity or weight control is excluded, save that bariatric surgery shall be
payable where the Body Mass Index of the Insured Person is greater than forty (40), or greater
than thirty-five (35) with an associated co-morbidity, and the surgery is advised in writing by
a Medical Practitioner.

6.3 Unproven Treatments
Expenses related to any treatment, including drugs and devices, which is not supported by
established medical practice and documented evidence of effectiveness, are excluded from this
Policy.

6.4 Breach of Law
Treatment for any Illness or Injury resulting from the Insured Person committing or attempting
to commit a breach of law with criminal intent is excluded.

SECTION 7. CLAIMS AND CONDITIONS

7.1 Claim Intimation
Intimation of a claim shall be given to the Insurer within twenty-four (24) hours of emergency
admission and at least seventy-two (72) hours before a planned admission.

7.2 Document Submission
Claim documents shall be submitted within fifteen (15) days of discharge. The Insurer may
condone a delay where the Insured Person proves to the satisfaction of the Insurer that the
delay was for reasons beyond their control.

7.3 Free Look Period
This Policy may be cancelled within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the Policy document. Where
the Policy has been sold through distance marketing, the free look period shall be thirty (30)
days.

7.4 Grace Period
A grace period of fifteen (15) days is allowed for renewal premium payment where premium is paid
annually, and thirty (30) days where premium is paid in instalments. Continuity of waiting
period credit is preserved if renewal occurs within the grace period.

7.5 No Claim Discount
A discount of five percent (5%) on the renewal premium shall be allowed for each claim-free
Policy Year, subject to a maximum discount of twenty-five percent (25%). This discount is
forfeited entirely in the event of a claim.
