MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED
DRIVESHIELD PRIVATE CAR PACKAGE POLICY

Product Type: Motor Private Car

SECTION 1. PREAMBLE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 Operative Clause
Meridian General Insurance Company Limited agrees, subject to the terms of this Policy, to
indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the Insured Vehicle and to cover the
Insured's legal liability to third parties arising out of the use of the Insured Vehicle during
the Policy Period.

1.2 Insured Declared Value
Insured Declared Value means the sum arrived at by applying the schedule of depreciation set out
in Clause 5.1 to the manufacturer's listed selling price of the vehicle and its accessories. The
Insured Declared Value shall be deemed to be the Sum Insured for the purposes of Section I of
this Policy and shall be the maximum amount payable in the event of total loss or constructive
total loss.

1.3 Constructive Total Loss
The Insured Vehicle shall be treated as a constructive total loss where the aggregate cost of
retrieval and repair, calculated at the time of loss, exceeds seventy-five percent (75%) of the
Insured Declared Value.

SECTION 2. SECTION I - LOSS OR DAMAGE TO THE INSURED VEHICLE

2.1 Perils Covered
The Company shall indemnify the Insured against loss or damage to the Insured Vehicle caused by
fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning; burglary, housebreaking or theft; riot and strike;
earthquake, flood, inundation, storm, cyclone and hailstorm; accidental external means;
malicious act; terrorist activity; and whilst in transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift,
elevator or air.

2.2 Towing Charges
The Company shall pay the reasonable cost of removal of the Insured Vehicle to the nearest
repairer following an accident, and the cost of reasonable repairs necessary to enable the
vehicle to reach that repairer, up to Rs. 1,500 in respect of any one accident.

2.3 Consumables Exclusion under Own Damage
Unless the Consumables Cover add-on has been opted for and is shown in the Policy Schedule, the
cost of engine oil, gearbox oil, coolant, brake fluid, nut and bolt, screw, washer, grease,
lubricant, clip, air-conditioner gas, wheel balancing weight and distilled water shall not be
payable in a partial loss claim.

SECTION 3. SECTION II - LIABILITY TO THIRD PARTIES

3.1 Third Party Death and Bodily Injury
The Company shall indemnify the Insured against all sums which the Insured becomes legally
liable to pay in respect of the death of or bodily injury to any third party caused by or
arising out of the use of the Insured Vehicle. The liability of the Company in respect of death
or bodily injury is unlimited in amount.

3.2 Third Party Property Damage
The Company's liability in respect of damage to third party property shall not exceed Rs.
7,50,000 in respect of any one accident, unless a higher limit has been opted for and the
corresponding premium has been paid.

3.3 Compulsory Personal Accident Cover for Owner-Driver
A compulsory personal accident cover of Rs. 15,00,000 is provided to the owner-driver whilst
driving the Insured Vehicle, including whilst mounting into, dismounting from or travelling in
the Insured Vehicle. The cover is subject to the owner-driver holding a valid and effective
driving licence at the time of the accident.

SECTION 4. ADD-ON COVERS

4.1 Zero Depreciation Cover
Where this add-on has been opted for, the Company shall not apply depreciation to the value of
parts replaced in a partial loss claim, except in respect of tyres, tubes and batteries, on
which fifty percent (50%) depreciation shall continue to apply. This add-on may be availed for a
maximum of two (2) claims in a Policy Year.

4.2 Engine Protect Cover
Where this add-on has been opted for, the Company shall indemnify the cost of repair or
replacement of the engine, gearbox and differential assembly consequent upon ingress of water
into the engine or leakage of lubricating oil, which would otherwise be excluded as
consequential loss. This cover does not apply where the Insured attempted to start the vehicle
after it was submerged in water.

4.3 Return to Invoice Cover
In the event of total loss, constructive total loss or theft of the Insured Vehicle, the Company
shall pay the difference between the Insured Declared Value and the current invoice value of a
new vehicle of the same make, model and variant, together with the first-time registration
charges and road tax. This add-on is available only for vehicles up to five (5) years of age.

4.4 Roadside Assistance
Where this add-on has been opted for, the Company shall arrange on-site minor repairs, flat tyre
assistance, battery jump start, emergency fuel delivery up to five (5) litres, key retrieval and
towing, available at any location within one hundred (100) kilometres of the Insured's
registered address. Availing this service shall not affect the No Claim Bonus.

SECTION 5. DEPRECIATION AND DEDUCTIBLES

5.1 Depreciation Schedule for Parts
In a partial loss claim, depreciation on the value of parts replaced shall be applied as
follows: for all rubber, nylon and plastic parts, tyres, tubes, batteries and air bags, fifty
percent (50%); for all parts made of fibre glass, thirty percent (30%); for all parts made of
glass, nil; and for all other parts, depreciation shall be applied according to the age of the
vehicle as set out in Clause 5.2.

5.2 Age-Based Depreciation on Metal Parts
For metal parts, depreciation shall be nil where the vehicle is not exceeding six (6) months
old; five percent (5%) where exceeding six months but not exceeding one year; ten percent (10%)
exceeding one year but not exceeding two years; fifteen percent (15%) exceeding two years but
not exceeding three years; twenty-five percent (25%) exceeding three years but not exceeding
four years; thirty-five percent (35%) exceeding four years but not exceeding five years; and
fifty percent (50%) exceeding five years.

5.3 Compulsory Deductible
A compulsory deductible of Rs. 1,000 shall apply to each and every own damage claim in respect
of private cars with an engine capacity not exceeding 1500 cc, and Rs. 2,000 where the engine
capacity exceeds 1500 cc. Any voluntary deductible opted for shall apply in addition to the
compulsory deductible.

SECTION 6. EXCLUSIONS

6.1 General Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable for consequential loss, depreciation, wear and tear, mechanical
or electrical breakdown, failures or breakages, damage to tyres and tubes unless the Insured
Vehicle is damaged at the same time, or loss or damage arising whilst the vehicle is used
outside the limitations as to use stated in the Policy Schedule.

6.2 Driving Without a Valid Licence
No claim shall be payable where, at the time of the accident, the vehicle was being driven by a
person not holding a valid and effective driving licence for the class of vehicle concerned, or
by a person who was disqualified from holding such a licence.

6.3 Driving Under the Influence
No claim shall be payable where the person driving the Insured Vehicle at the time of the
accident was under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs.

SECTION 7. CLAIMS AND CONDITIONS

7.1 Notice of Accident
Notice of any accident, loss or damage shall be given to the Company in writing immediately, and
in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of the occurrence. In the case of theft or criminal
act, the Insured shall also lodge a First Information Report with the police without delay and
provide a copy to the Company.

7.2 No Claim Bonus
Where no claim has been made or pending during the preceding Policy Period, a No Claim Bonus
shall be allowed on the own damage premium at renewal as follows: twenty percent (20%) after one
claim-free year, twenty-five percent (25%) after two consecutive claim-free years, thirty-five
percent (35%) after three, forty-five percent (45%) after four, and fifty percent (50%) after
five consecutive claim-free years.

7.3 Transfer of Interest
On transfer of ownership of the Insured Vehicle, the benefit of this Policy shall not pass to
the transferee unless the Company's consent in writing is obtained within fourteen (14) days of
the date of transfer. The No Claim Bonus earned by the previous owner shall not transfer and
remains to the credit of that owner.
