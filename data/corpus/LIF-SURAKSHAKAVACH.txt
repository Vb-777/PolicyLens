SECTION 1. PREAMBLE

1.1 Nature of Contract
Suraksha Kavach Term Assurance is a non-linked, non-participating, pure risk premium individual
life insurance plan. It acquires no surrender value, no paid-up value and no maturity value. The
Death Benefit is payable only on the death of the Life Assured during the Policy Term whilst the
Policy is in force.

1.2 Eligibility
The minimum age at entry is eighteen (18) years and the maximum age at entry is sixty-five (65)
years, both as on last birthday. The maximum maturity age is eighty-five (85) years. The minimum
Sum Assured is Rs. 25,00,000 and the Policy Term ranges from ten (10) to forty (40) years.

SECTION 2. BENEFITS

2.1 Death Benefit
On the death of the Life Assured during the Policy Term, provided the Policy is in force, the
Death Benefit payable shall be the highest of ten (10) times the annualised premium, one hundred
and five percent (105%) of all premiums paid up to the date of death, or the absolute Sum
Assured stated in the Policy Schedule.

2.2 Terminal Illness Benefit
Where the Life Assured is diagnosed with a Terminal Illness and is certified by two independent
Medical Practitioners as having a life expectancy not exceeding six (6) months, the Company
shall accelerate payment of fifty percent (50%) of the Sum Assured, subject to a maximum of Rs.
1,00,00,000. The Sum Assured payable on subsequent death shall stand reduced by the amount so
accelerated.

2.3 Death Benefit Payout Options
The Nominee may elect to receive the Death Benefit as a lump sum, as a level monthly income over
sixty (60), one hundred and twenty (120) or one hundred and eighty (180) months, or as a
combination of a lump sum of not less than twenty percent (20%) of the Death Benefit with the
balance paid as monthly income.

SECTION 3. RIDERS

3.1 Accidental Death Benefit Rider
Where this rider has been opted for, an additional amount equal to the Rider Sum Assured shall
be payable if the death of the Life Assured occurs as a direct result of an Accident, provided
death occurs within one hundred and eighty (180) days of the Accident. The Rider Sum Assured
shall not exceed the base Sum Assured.

3.2 Critical Illness Rider
Where this rider has been opted for, a lump sum equal to the Rider Sum Assured shall be payable
on first diagnosis of any one of the thirty-four (34) listed Critical Illnesses, subject to a
survival period of thirty (30) days from the date of diagnosis and a waiting period of ninety
(90) days from the rider commencement date.

SECTION 4. EXCLUSIONS

4.1 Suicide Clause
In the event of death of the Life Assured due to suicide within twelve (12) months from the date
of commencement of risk or from the date of revival of the Policy, whichever is later, the
Nominee shall be entitled to at least eighty percent (80%) of the total premiums paid up to the
date of death, or the surrender value available as at the date of death, whichever is higher,
provided the Policy is in force.

4.2 Rider Exclusions
No rider benefit shall be payable where the event arises from the Life Assured's participation
in any criminal act with unlawful intent, service in the armed forces during a period of war or
civil commotion, participation in aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger on a licensed
commercial airline, or participation in hazardous sports or racing of any kind.

SECTION 5. PREMIUM, GRACE, LAPSE AND REVIVAL

5.1 Grace Period
A grace period of thirty (30) days is allowed for payment of premium where the premium payment
mode is yearly, half-yearly or quarterly, and fifteen (15) days where the mode is monthly. The
Policy remains in force during the grace period.

5.2 Lapse
If premium is not paid before the expiry of the grace period, the Policy shall lapse from the
due date of the first unpaid premium and no benefit shall be payable thereafter, as this Policy
acquires no surrender value.

5.3 Revival
A lapsed Policy may be revived within five (5) years from the date of the first unpaid premium,
subject to payment of all arrears of premium together with interest at the rate declared by the
Company, and to the Life Assured furnishing satisfactory evidence of continued insurability at
their own cost.

5.4 Free Look Period
The Policyholder may return the Policy within thirty (30) days of receipt of the Policy document
if dissatisfied with its terms, and shall receive a refund of premium paid less proportionate
risk premium for the period of cover, medical examination expenses and stamp duty charges.

SECTION 6. CLAIMS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1 Claim Intimation and Documents
A death claim shall be intimated to the Company at the earliest and in any event within ninety
(90) days of the date of death. The Nominee shall submit the original Policy document, the death
certificate, the claimant's statement, proof of identity, and where applicable the post-mortem
report and police First Information Report.

6.2 Claim Settlement Timeline
Where no investigation is warranted, the Company shall settle a death claim within fifteen (15)
days of receipt of all required documents. Where an investigation is required, it shall be
completed within ninety (90) days of receipt of the claim intimation and the claim settled
within thirty (30) days thereafter.

6.3 Non-Disclosure and Section 45
No Policy shall be called in question on the ground of misstatement or suppression of material
fact after the expiry of three (3) years from the date of issuance of the Policy, the date of
commencement of risk, the date of revival, or the date of the rider to the Policy, whichever is
later, in accordance with Section 45 of the Insurance Act.
