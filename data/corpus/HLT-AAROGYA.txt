SECTION 1. PREAMBLE AND OPERATIVE CLAUSE

1.1 Contract of Insurance
This Policy is a contract of insurance issued by Meridian General Insurance Company Limited to
the Policyholder named in the Policy Schedule. The proposal form, the declarations made therein
and the Policy Schedule together form the basis of this contract. The Company agrees, subject to
the terms, conditions, exclusions and limitations contained herein, to indemnify the Insured
Person for Medical Expenses reasonably and necessarily incurred during the Policy Period, up to
the Sum Insured.

1.2 Territorial Scope and Governing Law
Cover under this Policy operates within the geographical limits of India. All claims shall be
payable in Indian Rupees. This Policy is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with
the laws of India, and the courts at the place of issuance shall have exclusive jurisdiction
over any dispute arising out of this Policy.

SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 Hospitalisation
Hospitalisation means admission of an Insured Person into a Hospital for a continuous period of
at least twenty-four (24) consecutive hours for Medically Necessary Treatment, save that this
minimum duration shall not apply to Day Care Treatment listed in Annexure A of this Policy.

2.2 Pre-Existing Disease
Pre-Existing Disease means any condition, ailment, injury or disease that is diagnosed by a
Medical Practitioner within forty-eight (48) months prior to the effective date of the first
Policy issued by the Company to the Insured Person, or for which medical advice or treatment was
recommended by or received from a Medical Practitioner within forty-eight (48) months prior to
that date.

2.3 Medically Necessary Treatment
Medically Necessary Treatment means any treatment, test, medication or stay in Hospital which is
required for the medical management of the Illness or Injury suffered by the Insured Person,
which is appropriate to the standard medical practice prevailing in India, which must not exceed
the level of care necessary to provide safe and adequate treatment, and which is prescribed by a
qualified Medical Practitioner.

2.4 Network Provider
Network Provider means a Hospital enlisted by the Company or by its Third Party Administrator to
provide medical services to Insured Persons on a cashless basis. The current list of Network
Providers is published on the Company's website and is subject to revision without prior notice.

SECTION 3. SCOPE OF COVER

3.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation Expenses
The Company shall indemnify Medical Expenses incurred on Hospitalisation of the Insured Person
during the Policy Period, including room, boarding and nursing charges, Intensive Care Unit
charges, surgeon, anaesthetist and specialist fees, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation
theatre charges, surgical appliances, medicines and drugs, and diagnostic procedures, up to the
Sum Insured specified in the Policy Schedule.

3.2 Pre-Hospitalisation and Post-Hospitalisation Expenses
Medical Expenses incurred during the sixty (60) days immediately preceding the date of
Hospitalisation and the ninety (90) days immediately following the date of discharge shall be
payable, provided the Hospitalisation claim itself has been admitted by the Company and the
expenses relate to the same condition.

3.3 Day Care Treatment
The Company shall indemnify expenses incurred on the Day Care Treatments listed in Annexure A
which, owing to technological advancement, require less than twenty-four (24) hours of
Hospitalisation. Treatment taken in the out-patient department of a Hospital shall not qualify
as Day Care Treatment under this clause.

3.4 Road Ambulance Charges
Expenses incurred on transportation of the Insured Person by road ambulance to a Hospital
following an Emergency, or between Hospitals for the purpose of obtaining Medically Necessary
Treatment, shall be reimbursed up to Rs. 5,000 per Hospitalisation event.

3.5 AYUSH Treatment
Medical Expenses incurred on in-patient treatment taken under Ayurveda, Yoga, Naturopathy,
Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy systems of medicine in a Hospital recognised by the relevant
statutory authority shall be indemnified up to twenty-five percent (25%) of the Sum Insured per
Policy Year.

3.6 Organ Donor Expenses
The Company shall indemnify Medical Expenses incurred towards the organ donor's Hospitalisation
for harvesting of the organ, provided the organ is for the use of the Insured Person and the
claim for the Insured Person's transplant has been admitted. Pre-harvesting screening expenses
of the donor and the cost of the organ itself are not payable.

SECTION 4. WAITING PERIODS

4.1 Initial Waiting Period
Save in respect of claims arising out of an Accident, the Company shall not be liable for any
expenses incurred in connection with Illness contracted during the first thirty (30) days from
the Policy Inception Date. This waiting period shall not apply to subsequent renewals of the
Policy without a break.

4.2 Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period
Expenses related to the treatment of a Pre-Existing Disease and its direct complications shall
be excluded until the expiry of thirty-six (36) months of continuous coverage after the date of
inception of the first Policy with the Company. Where the Insured Person has enhanced the Sum
Insured after inception, this waiting period shall apply afresh to the enhanced portion of the
Sum Insured.

4.3 Specified Disease and Procedure Waiting Period
A waiting period of twenty-four (24) months of continuous coverage shall apply to cataract,
benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia of all types, hydrocele, fissures and fistula in ano,
haemorrhoids, sinusitis, gall bladder and bile duct stone disease, varicose veins, and joint
replacement surgery other than that necessitated by an Accident.

4.4 Maternity Waiting Period
Maternity Expenses, including childbirth and lawful medical termination of pregnancy, shall be
payable only after the Insured Person has been continuously covered under this Policy for a
period of thirty-six (36) months. Maternity benefit is limited to two deliveries or terminations
during the lifetime of the Insured Person.

SECTION 5. SUB-LIMITS, DEDUCTIBLE AND CO-PAYMENT

5.1 Room Rent Sub-Limit
Room, boarding and nursing charges shall be payable up to one percent (1%) of the Sum Insured
per day for a normal room, and up to two percent (2%) of the Sum Insured per day for an
Intensive Care Unit. Where the Insured Person opts for a room category exceeding this
entitlement, all associated Medical Expenses shall be payable in the same proportion that the
entitled room rent bears to the actual room rent incurred.

5.2 Co-Payment
A co-payment of ten percent (10%) shall apply to each and every admissible claim where the
Insured Person was aged fifty-six (56) years or above on the date of first inception of the
Policy. No co-payment shall apply to Insured Persons who were below that age at first inception.

5.3 Cataract Sub-Limit
Expenses on treatment of cataract, once the applicable waiting period has expired, shall be
payable up to Rs. 40,000 per eye per Policy Year, inclusive of the cost of the intraocular lens.

5.4 Modern Treatment Methods
Expenses incurred on robotic surgery, stem cell therapy, oral chemotherapy, intra-vitreal
injections, bronchial thermoplasty, deep brain stimulation and immunotherapy shall be payable up
to fifty percent (50%) of the Sum Insured per Policy Year, subject to the treatment being taken
as in-patient or as listed Day Care Treatment.

SECTION 6. EXCLUSIONS

6.1 Cosmetic and Plastic Surgery
Expenses for cosmetic or plastic surgery or any treatment to change appearance are excluded,
unless such treatment is certified by the attending Medical Practitioner to be Medically
Necessary for reconstruction following an Accident, Burn or Cancer.

6.2 Hazardous Activities
Expenses related to any treatment necessitated due to participation as a professional in
hazardous or adventure sports, including para-jumping, rock climbing, mountaineering, rafting,
motor racing, horse racing, scuba diving, hand gliding, sky diving and deep-sea diving, are
excluded.

6.3 Substance Abuse and Self-Inflicted Injury
Expenses arising from or attributable to the abuse of alcohol or drugs, or treatment arising
from any intentionally self-inflicted injury, are excluded from this Policy.

6.4 Non-Medical Consumables
Items of personal comfort and convenience, including but not limited to television charges,
telephone charges, internet charges, attendant charges, toiletries, and administrative or
registration fees, are not payable. A complete schedule of non-payable items is set out in
Annexure B.

6.5 Out-Patient Treatment
Expenses on treatment taken in the out-patient department of a Hospital or a clinic, including
consultations, diagnostics and pharmacy purchases not connected with an admissible
Hospitalisation, are excluded unless an out-patient benefit has been specifically opted for and
is shown in the Policy Schedule.

6.6 War and Nuclear Perils
The Company shall not be liable for any claim arising directly or indirectly out of war,
invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, revolution, military or
usurped power, or from nuclear weapons material or ionising radiation.

SECTION 7. CLAIMS PROCEDURE

7.1 Notification of Claim
In the event of planned Hospitalisation, the Company or its Third Party Administrator shall be
notified at least forty-eight (48) hours prior to admission. In the event of emergency
Hospitalisation, notification shall be given within twenty-four (24) hours of admission.

7.2 Submission of Documents
All documents in support of a reimbursement claim, including the original discharge summary,
hospital bills, payment receipts, investigation reports and the duly completed claim form, shall
be submitted to the Company within thirty (30) days of the date of discharge from the Hospital.

7.3 Cashless Facility
Cashless facility is available only at Network Providers. A pre-authorisation request shall be
submitted by the Hospital to the Third Party Administrator, who shall respond with an approval,
query or denial within four (4) hours of receiving a complete request. Denial of
pre-authorisation does not constitute denial of the claim, and the Insured Person may file for
reimbursement.

7.4 Settlement and Interest
The Company shall settle or reject a claim within thirty (30) days of receipt of the last
necessary document. Where settlement is delayed beyond this period, the Company shall pay
interest at a rate two percent (2%) above the prevailing bank rate from the date of receipt of
the last necessary document until the date of payment.

SECTION 8. GENERAL CONDITIONS

8.1 Free Look Period
The Insured Person may cancel this Policy within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the Policy
document, stating the reasons for cancellation, and shall be refunded the premium paid less any
expenses incurred by the Company on medical examination and stamp duty. The free look period
shall be thirty (30) days where the Policy has been purchased through distance marketing.

8.2 Grace Period
A grace period of thirty (30) days from the Policy expiry date is allowed for payment of renewal
premium without loss of continuity benefits such as waiting periods and coverage of Pre-Existing
Diseases. Coverage shall not be available for the period during which no premium has been
received.

8.3 Cumulative Bonus
For every claim-free Policy Year, the Sum Insured shall be increased by ten percent (10%) of the
base Sum Insured, subject to a maximum accumulation of fifty percent (50%). In the event of a
claim, the accrued cumulative bonus shall be reduced by ten percent (10%) of the base Sum
Insured at the next renewal.

8.4 Portability
The Insured Person may port this Policy to another insurer at renewal by applying to the new
insurer at least forty-five (45) days but not earlier than sixty (60) days before the renewal
date. Credit gained for waiting periods shall be transferred to the extent of the Sum Insured
under the expiring policy.

8.5 Moratorium Period
After sixty (60) continuous months of coverage have elapsed since inception of the first Policy,
no claim shall be contestable by the Company on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation,
except on grounds of established fraud. This moratorium shall apply to the Sum Insured of the
first Policy, and separately to any enhanced Sum Insured from the date of such enhancement.

8.6 Grievance Redressal
Any grievance may be addressed to the Grievance Officer of the Company, who shall respond within
fifteen (15) days of receipt. If the response is unsatisfactory, or if no response is received,
the Insured Person may approach the Insurance Ombudsman having jurisdiction.
