SECTION 1. PREAMBLE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 Operative Clause
Meridian General Insurance Company Limited agrees to indemnify the Insured in respect of loss of
or damage to the Insured Property described in the Policy Schedule occurring during the Policy
Period, subject to the Sums Insured, terms, conditions and exclusions of each Section opted for.

1.2 Reinstatement Value
Reinstatement Value means the cost of replacing or reinstating property of the same kind or type
by new property, without deduction for depreciation. Building cover under Section A operates on
a Reinstatement Value basis, whilst Contents cover under Section B operates on an Indemnity
basis with depreciation applied, unless the Reinstatement Value option has been opted for.

SECTION 2. SECTION A - BUILDING

2.1 Perils Covered
The Company shall indemnify loss or damage to the building caused by fire, lightning, explosion
or implosion, aircraft damage, riot, strike and malicious damage, storm, cyclone, flood and
inundation, impact damage by any rail or road vehicle, subsidence and landslide including
rockslide, bursting or overflowing of water tanks and pipes, missile testing operations, leakage
from automatic sprinkler installations, and bush fire.

2.2 Architects and Surveyors Fees
Fees of architects, surveyors and consulting engineers necessarily incurred in the reinstatement
of the building following an admitted claim shall be payable up to three percent (3%) of the
claim amount.

2.3 Debris Removal
The reasonable cost of removal of debris from the insured premises following an admitted claim
shall be payable up to two percent (2%) of the claim amount.

2.4 Loss of Rent and Alternative Accommodation
Where the insured building is rendered uninhabitable following an admitted claim, the Company
shall pay the rent for alternative accommodation of a similar standard for the period reasonably
required for reinstatement, up to a maximum of twelve (12) months and not exceeding ten percent
(10%) of the Sum Insured on the building.

SECTION 3. SECTION B - CONTENTS

3.1 Scope
Household contents, furniture, fixtures, fittings, domestic appliances and personal effects
belonging to the Insured or to any member of the Insured's family permanently residing with them
are covered against the perils listed in Clause 2.1, up to the Sum Insured for Section B.

3.2 Valuables Sub-Limit
Jewellery, precious stones, articles of gold and silver, watches, curios and works of art are
covered only up to twenty-five percent (25%) of the Section B Sum Insured, or Rs. 2,00,000,
whichever is lower, unless specifically declared and listed in the Policy Schedule with
valuation certificates.

3.3 Single Article Limit
In respect of contents that have not been specifically declared, the liability of the Company
for any one article shall not exceed five percent (5%) of the Section B Sum Insured.

SECTION 4. SECTION C - BURGLARY AND THEFT

4.1 Scope
Loss of or damage to contents by burglary, housebreaking or theft following actual forcible and
violent entry into or exit from the insured premises, including damage to the premises caused in
the course of such entry, is covered up to the Section C Sum Insured.

4.2 Unoccupancy Condition
Cover under Section C shall be suspended where the insured premises remain unoccupied for a
continuous period exceeding thirty (30) days, unless prior written notice has been given to the
Company and the Company has agreed in writing to continue cover.

SECTION 5. SECTION D - PORTABLE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT

5.1 Scope and Territorial Limit
Portable electronic equipment including laptops, tablets, cameras and mobile telephones
belonging to the Insured is covered against accidental damage and theft anywhere in India, and
worldwide for a maximum of sixty (60) days in any Policy Year whilst the Insured is travelling.

5.2 Depreciation on Portable Equipment
In the event of a total loss of portable electronic equipment, depreciation shall be applied at
ten percent (10%) per completed year from the date of purchase, subject to a maximum of fifty
percent (50%). Theft from an unattended vehicle is not covered unless the equipment was in a
locked boot and there is evidence of forcible entry.

SECTION 6. EXCLUSIONS AND CONDITIONS

6.1 General Exclusions
This Policy does not cover loss or damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, moth,
vermin, insects, rust, mildew or dampness; loss of cash, bank notes, securities or documents
unless specifically covered; property held in trust or on commission unless declared; or loss
arising from war, nuclear perils or wilful act of the Insured.

6.2 Underinsurance and Average
Where at the time of loss the Sum Insured is less than the value of the insured property, the
Insured shall be considered their own insurer for the difference and shall bear a rateable
proportion of the loss. This condition of average applies separately to each Section and to each
item of the Policy Schedule.

6.3 Claim Notification
The Insured shall notify the Company of any event likely to give rise to a claim within seven
(7) days of its occurrence, and in the case of burglary, theft or malicious damage, shall also
lodge a complaint with the police within twenty-four (24) hours.
