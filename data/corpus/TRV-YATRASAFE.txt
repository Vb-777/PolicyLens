SECTION 1. PREAMBLE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 Scope of Contract
Sentinel Health Assurance Limited agrees to provide the benefits described in this Policy in
respect of events occurring during a Trip undertaken by the Insured Person outside the Republic
of India within the Policy Period, subject to the limits stated in the Schedule of Benefits.

1.2 Trip
Trip means a journey commencing when the Insured Person crosses the Indian immigration outbound
counter and ending on the earlier of the Insured Person clearing Indian immigration on return,
or the expiry of the Policy Period. The maximum duration of any single Trip shall be one hundred
and eighty (180) days.

SECTION 2. MEDICAL BENEFITS

2.1 Emergency Medical Expenses
The Company shall indemnify expenses necessarily incurred outside India on emergency medical
treatment of an Illness contracted or Injury sustained during the Trip, up to the Sum Insured
stated in the Schedule, subject to a deductible of USD 100 per claim. Treatment of a condition
known to exist before the Trip commenced is not covered save as provided in Clause 2.3.

2.2 Emergency Medical Evacuation and Repatriation
Where the attending Medical Practitioner and the Company's assistance provider jointly determine
that the Insured Person must be evacuated to the nearest facility capable of providing adequate
care, or repatriated to India, the Company shall arrange and pay for such evacuation or
repatriation up to USD 50,000. Repatriation of mortal remains to India is covered up to USD
10,000.

2.3 Pre-Existing Condition in Life-Threatening Emergency
Notwithstanding Clause 2.1, expenses incurred on immediate relief of a Pre-Existing Condition
which manifests as a life-threatening emergency during the Trip shall be payable up to USD
10,000, limited to such treatment as is necessary to stabilise the Insured Person for return to
India.

2.4 Dental Treatment Abroad
Emergency dental treatment necessitated by acute pain or by an Accident during the Trip shall be
payable up to USD 500. Routine dental care, crowns, bridges and orthodontic work are excluded.

SECTION 3. TRAVEL INCONVENIENCE BENEFITS

3.1 Trip Cancellation
Where the Trip is cancelled before departure owing to the death, serious Illness or Injury of
the Insured Person or an Immediate Family Member, or owing to the Insured Person being summoned
for jury service or as a witness, the Company shall reimburse irrecoverable prepaid travel and
accommodation costs up to USD 1,500.

3.2 Trip Delay
Where the scheduled departure of a common carrier is delayed by more than six (6) hours owing to
inclement weather, equipment failure or strike action, the Company shall pay USD 50 for each
full six-hour block of delay, up to a maximum of USD 300.

3.3 Checked-In Baggage Loss
In the event of permanent loss of checked-in baggage by a common carrier, the Company shall pay
up to USD 1,000, subject to a maximum of USD 500 per bag and USD 100 per individual item. Any
compensation received from the carrier shall be deducted from the amount payable.

3.4 Baggage Delay
Where checked-in baggage is delayed by more than twelve (12) hours from the time of arrival at
the destination, the Company shall reimburse the cost of necessary emergency purchases up to USD
200, supported by original receipts and written confirmation of the delay from the carrier.

3.5 Loss of Passport
The Company shall reimburse the actual expenses necessarily incurred in obtaining a duplicate or
fresh passport following its loss abroad, up to USD 300, provided the loss was reported to the
local police within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

SECTION 4. PERSONAL LIABILITY AND ACCIDENT

4.1 Personal Liability
The Company shall indemnify the Insured Person against legal liability to third parties for
accidental bodily injury or accidental damage to property occurring during the Trip, up to USD
1,00,000. Liability arising out of the ownership or use of any motor vehicle, aircraft or
watercraft, or arising from any contractual undertaking, is excluded.

4.2 Personal Accident
In the event of death or permanent total disablement of the Insured Person caused by an Accident
during the Trip, the Company shall pay the Sum Insured for this benefit as stated in the
Schedule. Permanent partial disablement shall be payable according to the scale of benefits in
Annexure C.

SECTION 5. EXCLUSIONS AND CLAIMS

5.1 General Exclusions
No benefit shall be payable in respect of any claim arising from travel undertaken against
medical advice, travel for the purpose of obtaining medical treatment, participation in
professional sports, manual labour or hazardous occupation, pregnancy or childbirth, mental or
nervous disorders, or travel to a country for which the Government of India has issued a travel
advisory against travel.

5.2 Claim Notification
All claims shall be notified to the Company's twenty-four hour assistance helpline as soon as
reasonably practicable and in any event within seven (7) days of the event. Written claim
documents shall be submitted within thirty (30) days of return to India.

5.3 Extension of Policy Period
The Policy Period may be extended once, for a maximum of one hundred and eighty (180) additional
days, provided the request is made before the expiry of the original Policy Period, no claim has
been made or is pending, and the additional premium is paid.
